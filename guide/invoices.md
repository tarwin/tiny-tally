# Invoices, quotes and credit notes

![Invoices, with what's overdue](/shots/invoices.webp)

## Making an invoice

**New invoice** (<kbd>⇧⌘I</kbd>), then pick the customer and add lines. Leave the number blank and the next one is used. You can also set:

- **Reference**: their PO number, say.
- **Payment terms**: due on receipt, 7, 14 or 30 days, end of next month, or a date of your own.
- **Prices are** GST inclusive or exclusive.
- **From**: which [trading name](./templates#trading-names) it comes from, and its **Template**.
- **Notes**.

**Add saved item…** adds one of your [products and services](./contacts#products-and-services); **Save as item** keeps a line for next time.

![Editing an invoice](/shots/invoice-editor.webp)

**Approve & save** makes it real: it gets its number and counts in your reports. **Save as draft** keeps it to finish later. Drafts don't count anywhere.

## Sending it

![An invoice, with its payments beside it](/shots/invoice.webp)

- **Print / Save PDF** makes the PDF.
- **Email** opens a message to the customer in your mail app, with the details filled in. Attach the PDF and send it yourself; Tiny Tally never sends anything.
- Once it's overdue, the button becomes **Send reminder** and drafts a polite nudge.

An invoice is *Draft*, *Awaiting payment*, *Part paid*, *Overdue*, *Paid* or *Void*. The sidebar counts the overdue ones.

## Getting paid

**Record payment** with the date, amount and the account it went into. Part payments are fine; the invoice shows what's left. Payments also appear in that account's register.

## Quotes

Quotes work the same way, with **Valid for** instead of payment terms. A quote is *Draft*, *Sent*, *Expired*, *Accepted* or *Declined*. **Accept & invoice** turns it into an invoice with the same lines. A declined quote can be reopened.

## Credit notes

To take back some or all of an invoice, choose **Credit note** on it. A credit note can be **applied** to that invoice or another one for the same customer, or **refunded** as money paid back.

## More on an invoice

- **Make recurring**: send the same invoice on a schedule. See [Recurring](./recurring).
- **Duplicate**: start a new one from this one.
- **Void**: cancel a sent invoice but keep its number and record.
- **History**: every change, by whom and when.
- **Statement**: everything with that customer. See [Contacts](./contacts#statements).
