# Money accounts and reconciling

**Money accounts** in the sidebar lists where your money is: **bank accounts**, **credit cards** and **cash**.

## Adding an account

**Add account**, then give it a **Name**, its **Type**, the date to **Start tracking from**, and the **Balance on that date** (for a card, the amount owed). Start from a date you have a statement for, so the balance is easy to check.

An account you've closed can be archived; it drops out of lists but its history stays. **Show archived** brings it back into view.

## The register

Click an account to see its register: every transaction in and out, with the running balance. It has its own New expense, New income and New transfer (*Pay off card* for a credit card), and **Export CSV**.

## Reconciling {#reconcile}

Reconciling checks your books against the bank's statement, so you know nothing's missing or doubled up.

![Reconciling an account](/shots/reconcile.webp)

1. Choose **Reconcile** on the account.
2. Enter the **Statement end date** and the **Closing balance on the statement** (for a card, the amount owed), then **Start reconciling**.
3. Tick each transaction that's on the statement. **Tick all shown** helps when everything's there; **Match statement file…** ticks them for you from the bank's OFX, QBO, CSV or Excel file and lists anything it couldn't match, with an **Add** button for each.
4. When **Difference** is zero, **Finish**.

Something on the statement missing from your books? **Add missing transaction**. Changed your mind? **Stop** clears your ticks; **Change statement** fixes the date or balance.

Reconciled transactions get a tick in your lists. **Past reconciliations** lists the earlier ones, and the most recent can be undone.
