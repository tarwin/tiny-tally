# Reports

**Reports** in the sidebar has everything, with this period's tax so far at the top.

![The reports page](/shots/reports.webp)

Most reports take a **Period** (this month, last month, this quarter, this or last financial year, or your own dates) and have **Print / Save PDF** and **Export CSV**.

## Profit & Loss

Income and expenses for a period, by category. **Basis** is *Cash* (when money moved) or *Accrual* (when you invoiced or were billed). **Compare with** the previous period or your [budget](./budget).

![Profit and loss against the budget](/shots/profit-and-loss.webp)

## Balance Sheet

What the business has and owes on a date, and the owner's equity.

![The balance sheet](/shots/balance-sheet.webp)

## Cash flow

Where cash came from and went: day-to-day trading, buying and selling assets, and owner and loan money.

![The cash flow statement](/shots/cash-flow.webp)

## And

- **Sales by customer** and **Expenses by supplier**, with the previous period beside them.
- **Who owes you** and **Who you owe**: unpaid invoices and bills by age.
- **Journal & Trial Balance**, and **Journal entries**. See [For your accountant](./accountants).
- Your tax report. See [Tax returns](./tax).
