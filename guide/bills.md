# Bills

Bills are what your suppliers send you. Recording them, rather than just the payment, means you can see what you owe and when it's due.

![Bills to pay](/shots/bills.webp)

**New bill** (<kbd>⇧⌘B</kbd>) works like an invoice: the supplier, their **Supplier's invoice number**, the due date and the lines, each with a category. Attach the PDF they sent.

**Record payment** when you pay it. A paid bill has a **Remittance advice** to print or email, so the supplier knows what the payment was for.

Overdue bills are counted in the sidebar, and **Who you owe** in Reports lists them by how long they've been owing. Bills can be [recurring](./recurring) too.
