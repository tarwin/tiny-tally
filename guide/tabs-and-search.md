# Tabs, windows and search

## Tabs

Look at several things at once in the same window: an invoice, the customer's statement and the bank register, say.

![Three tabs on the same books](/shots/tabs.webp)

- **New Tab**: <kbd>⌘T</kbd>, the **+** in the tab strip, or a double-click on an empty part of the strip.
- **Open a link in a new tab**: <kbd>⌘</kbd>-click it (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>-click on Windows and Linux), or middle-click. Add <kbd>⇧</kbd> to open it behind the current tab.
- **Switch**: click a tab, press <kbd>⌘1</kbd> to <kbd>⌘9</kbd>, or <kbd>⇧⌘]</kbd> and <kbd>⇧⌘[</kbd> for the next and previous.
- **Close**: <kbd>⌘W</kbd>, the × on the tab, or middle-click it.
- **Reorder**: drag a tab along the strip.
- Right-click a tab for **Close Other Tabs** and **Move Tab to New Window**.

Each tab remembers where it's been, so Back and Forward (<kbd>⌘[</kbd> and <kbd>⌘]</kbd>) work per tab. A tab you come back to is as you left it, filters and all. Your tabs are remembered for each set of books.

## Windows

**New Window** (<kbd>⌘N</kbd>) opens another window on the same books, at the page you're on. It's handy across two screens. You can also drag a tab out of the strip to give it its own window.

Every tab and window on the same books stays up to date: record a payment in one and the invoice list in another changes too.

To work on a different set of books at the same time, use **Open Books…** (<kbd>⌘O</kbd>). Each set of books opens in its own window. Give each one its own **Colour** in Settings so you can tell them apart.

## Search {#search}

<kbd>⌘K</kbd> (or click **Search** at the top of the window) finds:

- pages and actions, like *New invoice* or *Rules*
- transactions, invoices, quotes, credit notes and bills, by description, number, contact, reference or total
- contacts, accounts and categories

![Searching for "office"](/shots/search.webp)

Type an amount, like `89.99` or `$1,234.50`, to find that exact amount.

The last result, *All transactions matching…*, opens the Transactions page with that search.
