# Recurring transactions

Rent, subscriptions, a monthly retainer: anything that repeats can be set up once.

![Recurring schedules](/shots/recurring.webp)

Make one from:

- a transaction, with **Repeat this** in the editor
- an invoice or bill, with **Make recurring**

A schedule **Repeats** every week, 2 weeks, month, 3 months or year, from the **First one**, and can **Stop after** a date.

Whatever has fallen due is created when you open your books, so nothing is missed if Tiny Tally was closed. A message tells you what was made. **Create due now** does it straight away.

For invoices and bills, **Approve each invoice automatically** decides whether each new one goes out approved or waits as a draft for you to check.

The **Recurring** page lists them all. Each schedule can be edited, have its next one skipped (**Skip next**), be paused and resumed, or deleted. Deleting a schedule leaves what it already created.
