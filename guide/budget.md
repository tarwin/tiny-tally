# Budget

**Budget** sets what you expect to earn and spend in each category, month by month, for a financial year.

![The budget, filled in from last year](/shots/budget.webp)

The quick way: **Start from last year** fills in last year's actual figures. Adjust anything you expect to change, then **Save**.

To see how you're going, **Compare on Profit & Loss** opens the Profit & Loss with your budget and the difference beside each category.
