## Changing shortcuts {#changing-shortcuts}

Open **Help › Keyboard Shortcuts…**, or **Change shortcuts…** in App settings. Click a shortcut, then press the keys you want: <kbd>⌘</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>) with a letter, number or punctuation key, plus <kbd>⇧</kbd> or <kbd>⌥</kbd> (<kbd>Alt</kbd>) if you like. <kbd>Delete</kbd> removes a shortcut and <kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels.

If the keys already belong to another command, they move to the new one and the other is left without a shortcut. Copy, paste, undo, select all, quit, hide and <kbd>⌘1</kbd>–<kbd>⌘9</kbd> can't be changed. The circular arrow puts one back to the default, and **Reset all** puts back every one. Your shortcuts apply to every window and every set of books.
