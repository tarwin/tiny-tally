# Settings

There are two kinds: settings for these books, which are kept in the books file, and settings for the app on this computer.

## Books settings

**Settings** in the sidebar (<kbd>⌥⌘,</kbd>):

- **Your name**, financial year start, tax ID, **Logo**, and a **Colour** for the sidebar, to tell sets of books apart. The country can't be changed.
- How amounts are shown in the app and on documents.
- **Trading names**. See [Templates and trading names](./templates#trading-names).
- **Tax**: whether you're registered, how often you report, cash or accrual, and whether invoice prices include GST by default.
- **Invoices**: prefixes and next numbers for each kind of document.
- **Lock date**. See [Locking past periods](./your-books-file#locking-past-periods).
- **Books file**: show it in Finder, back up now, or close the books.

## App settings

<kbd>⌘,</kbd> on a Mac, or File → **App Settings…** on Windows and Linux:

- **Theme**: System, Light or Dark.
- **Density**: Compact, Regular or Spacious. **Size**: 90% to 125%.
- **Collapse the sidebar to icons**.
- **Open the last books when Tiny Tally starts**.

Theme and density are also in the View menu.

## Updates

Tiny Tally checks for a new version when it starts and once a day. When one's ready you'll see **Tiny Tally X is ready**: choose **Restart and update**, or **Later**. **Check for Updates…** is in the Tiny Tally menu on a Mac, and the Help menu on Windows and Linux.
