# For your accountant

Underneath, Tiny Tally keeps a proper double-entry ledger. Every transaction, invoice, bill and payment posts balanced debits and credits, so the reports an accountant expects are all there.

## Reports they'll want

- **Journal & Trial Balance**: every posting as a general journal, or the trial balance on a date.
- **Profit & Loss** on a cash or accrual basis, and the **Balance Sheet**.
- Your tax return, with the transactions behind each figure.

Each one can be printed, saved as PDF or exported to CSV. They can also just open your `.tally` file in Tiny Tally themselves.

## Journal entries

For adjustments that aren't an ordinary transaction, such as depreciation, accruals or corrections, use **Journal entries** in the sidebar. **New journal** takes a date, a description and any number of lines, each with an account, a debit or credit and a note. It can't be saved until it balances.

**Reverse it on** a date creates the opposite entry then, for accruals that undo themselves next period.

## The audit trail

Every change is logged with when and on which computer, and can be looked back on in **Activity**. See [History](./your-books-file#history). The [lock date](./your-books-file#locking-past-periods) keeps finished periods from being changed.
